# Working on Tropical Island

How to run the project from its source, build the app, the manual and the disk image, and where
things are. What the island holds is in [island.md](island.md).

## Running it

- **App**: open `build/Tropical Island.app`. It is a self-contained macOS application (for
  Apple silicon only, ad-hoc signed, no Godot needed). The menu bar has *About Tropical Island* and under it
  *Licenses* (the notices of Godot and its parts), *Help › Manual* (the PDF field guide, which lies
  inside the app) and *Help › Keyboard Shortcuts*. Rebuild it with `zsh tools/build_app.sh`.
- **Release**: `build/Tropical-Island-1.0-macos-arm64.dmg`, a disk image with the app, a link to the
  Applications folder and the manual: open it, drag the app onto Applications. Rebuild it with
  `zsh tools/build_dmg.sh` (after the app or the manual has changed; it needs a Python with
  `pillow` and `ds_store`, and takes the manual's environment if there is one:
  `manual/venv/bin/pip install pillow ds_store`). The app is not notarized:
  on another Mac the first start is refused, and has to be allowed once in *System Settings ›
  Privacy & Security* ("Open Anyway"; on macOS 14 and earlier right-click › Open does it).
- **Editor**: open this folder in Godot 4.7.2 and press **⌘ B** (main scene `scenes/main.tscn`).
  The game runs in its own window ("Embed Game on Next Play" is off), so fullscreen works.

## Manual

`manual/Tropical Island - Manual.pdf` (28 pages, A4): how to play, a map of the island, the
day, moon, tide, rain and the glowing sea, and a field guide to the 91 plants and animals
with a picture of each (all taken in the game) and a box to tick. Its words are in
`manual/content.py`, its pictures in `manual/images/` (cut by `prep_images.py` from the
screenshots in `manual/sources/`); rebuild it with `python build_manual.py` in `manual/`
(needs `reportlab` and `pillow`: `python3 -m venv venv && venv/bin/pip install reportlab pillow`,
then `venv/bin/python build_manual.py`). It describes the game as built: when a species, a key
or a cycle changes, the manual changes with it. The app holds a copy (*Help › Manual*), so
build the app again after the manual, then the disk image.

## Orientation

The start beach is the island's **west** coast: the sun sets over its sea and rises behind
the island. In the game's coordinates **+x is west, -x east, +z north, -z south** (y up);
the island runs north to south. (Until 2026-11-12 the notes and the code called the start
beach the east coast; everything was renamed then.)

## The README's pictures

`docs/readme/` holds the pictures of the front page, drawn by `python3 tools/readme_art.py`
(Pillow) from the manual's pictures and two screenshots in `docs/readme/source/`. The names
under the species come from `manual/content.py`: run it again when the manual changes.
`python3 tools/readme_preview.py` writes a rough preview of `README.md` to
`build/readme_preview.html` (GitHub's own rendering is the one that counts).

## How it was made

- Models: exported from the original .blend and generated by Python scripts in Blender
  (`tools/island.py`, `tools/assets2–7.py`); the .blend itself was never modified. The final shape
  of the island and the grotto come from two plain Python scripts (`tools/terrain2.py`,
  `tools/grotto2.py`).
- Textures and the icon: Z Image Turbo (Apache 2.0); sounds: Stable Audio 3 Medium (Stability AI
  Community License: outputs belong to who made them; free for non-commercial use, commercial
  use needs registering with Stability).
- The engine is under the MIT license, the `godot_ai` editor addon too (`addons/godot_ai/LICENSE`).
  Their notices and those of the engine's third-party parts are in the app (*Tropical Island › Licenses*)
  and in `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt` (written by `tools/licenses_txt.gd` at every build).
  The typefaces of the manual, of the README's pictures and of the disk image's backdrop are
  Nunito Sans (The Nunito Sans Project Authors) and PT Serif (ParaType), both under the SIL Open
  Font License 1.1: the files are in `manual/fonts/` as their makers publish them, each with its
  license text.
- Engine: Godot 4.7.2 (Forward+, Jolt physics). Idea and direction by Hanno Teeling.
  Concept extension and initial implementation by Claude Opus 5.5. Further substantial
  extension and refinement by Claude Fable 5.2.

## Layout

- `scenes/main.tscn`: the scene; `scripts/`: world, landscape placement, player, animals, sound,
  time of day, menus; `shaders/`: ocean, sand, sky, foliage, rock, animals.
- `assets/models`: glTF models and small JSON data; `assets/textures`, `assets/audio`.
- `tools/`: Blender asset scripts, audio/texture/icon processing, `build_app.sh`, `build_dmg.sh`, and the test
  driver (`shot.gd`) with its helpers.
- `manual/`: the player's manual (PDF), its text, pictures, typefaces (`fonts/`) and the scripts that took the pictures.
- `dmg/`: the picture behind the icons of the release's disk image, the script that draws it
  (`make_background.py`, which also holds the window's layout) and the one that writes that layout
  into the image (`fix_layout.py`).
- `docs/`: this file, the island in words, and the README's pictures.
- Not in the public repository: the working notes (`HANDOFF.md`), the ComfyUI workflows
  (`comfyui_workflows/`) and the work sheets of screenshots (`screenshots/`). Some comments in
  the code refer to them.
